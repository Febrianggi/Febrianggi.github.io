RINGKASAN: A scoping review of 35 articles on calibrating ensemble rainfall forecasts with
Bayesian Model Averaging, following PRISMA-ScR and paired with a bibliometric analysis.
Gaussian-based approaches still dominate, while the Gamma distribution, better suited to
skewed rainfall data, remains little studied.
