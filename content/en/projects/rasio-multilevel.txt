JUDUL: Multilevel Analysis Dashboard
SUBJUDUL: Factors behind students' mathematics achievement, RASIO 10.0 final round

RINGKASAN: The dashboard from the final round of the Padjadjaran Statistics Olympiad
(RASIO). The question: is the variation in students' math scores mostly down to the
students themselves, or also to the school they attend?

METODE: The organizers' survey data cover 900 students drawn from 30 schools
(30 students per school), so the data are hierarchical: students nested in schools.
Students in the same school tend to score alike, so they cannot be treated as
independent observations.

Linear Mixed Models were therefore built step by step from M1 to M4: variance
decomposition, then student traits (prior score, socioeconomic status, study hours),
school traits (facility quality, teacher experience), and a study-hours slope allowed
to vary across schools, followed by model diagnostics.
