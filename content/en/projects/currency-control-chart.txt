SUBJUDUL: A dashboard of the rupiah's performance against world currencies

RINGKASAN: An interactive dashboard that places the rupiah's rate per SDR on an
x̄ control chart, then compares it with other currencies worldwide and in ASEAN.
Visitors can pick a country, a period, and the underlying data tables.

METODE: Monthly exchange rates per SDR from April 2021 to April 2026 (61 months)
were processed in Google Colab. The center line is the mean rate, and the upper and
lower control limits sit 2 standard deviations either side of it.

The latest month is then placed against the control limits, with the 12 months
before it shown as reference dots on the same bar.

HASIL:
- In April 2026 one SDR cost Rp 23,757, up 653 from the month before.
- That is above the upper control limit of Rp 23,186 (center Rp 20,985, lower limit Rp 18,784).
- Of the 61 months observed, 1 fell outside the control limits.
