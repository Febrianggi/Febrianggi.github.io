JUDUL: PCA and K-Means Clustering
SUBJUDUL: Grouping countries with World Happiness Report data

RINGKASAN: Countries in the World Happiness Report are grouped with K-Means after
the data are reduced with Principal Component Analysis. The whole analysis is an R script.

METODE: The World Happiness Report variables are first reduced with PCA so the
grouping is not dominated by correlated variables. The principal component scores
are then clustered with K-Means into two clusters.

HASIL:
- Indonesia falls in Cluster 1, alongside Egypt, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, and Peru among others.
- Cluster 2 includes Singapore, Norway, Switzerland, Malaysia, and Chile among others.
